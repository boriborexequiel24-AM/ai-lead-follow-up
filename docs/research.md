# Research: most requested business automations

Done on 2026-11-17. There is no neutral, large-scale ranking. Most sources are vendors reporting on their own customers, so treat the order as a strong hint, not a measurement.

## Findings
| Automation | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Lead capture and follow-up | Top small-business workflow in a [DEV Community guide](https://dev.to/jahanzaibai/how-to-automate-your-business-workflow-what-actually-works-in-2026-12id) and a [lowcode.agency guide](https://www.lowcode.agency/blog/how-to-build-an-ai-automation-builder-tailored-for-small-businesses). A captured lead is reported as the most common output across [20 million live workflows](https://stacker.com/stories/business-economy/most-common-business-automation-use-cases-2026-according-20-million-live) (full ranking not verified). |
| Invoicing, payments, order-to-cash | Ranked first as the task UK small business owners most want automated (survey of 167, via [AI Agency Plus](https://aiagencyplus.com/what-business-processes-can-you-automate-with-ai/)). Most automated workflows among 700+ IT leaders per [Celigo](https://www.celigo.com/blog/automation-best-practices/). |
| Reporting and system connectivity | Top two for mid-market and larger companies in [Parabola's 2026 report](https://parabola.io/reports/the-2026-automation-report). |
| Support triage and reply drafting | A common safe first AI step ([Paperform](https://paperform.co/blog/automation-statistics/)). |
| Onboarding, HR, scheduling | Common but lower down ([Kissflow](https://kissflow.com/workflow/workflow-automation-statistics-trends/?app=wp)). |

## Caveats
- Enterprise sources lean toward back-office integration; small-business sources lean toward leads and invoices.
- Process design matters more than the tool. [N-iX](https://www.n-ix.com/enterprise-automation-growing-trends/) reports 61% of businesses say their automation tools are underused.
- The Bain Automation and AI Pathfinder Survey 2026 (n=951) looks most rigorous but its process-level ranking was not checked.
